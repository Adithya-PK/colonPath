## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

Phase 6 established end-to-end clinical decision-support quality, grounded explainability, evidence traceability, and safe clinical language across the entire **COLONPATH-AI V2** platform.

Every explanation, clinical summary, Copilot response, and PDF report generated by the system is now:
1. **Fully Traceable**: Linked to atomic `evidence_trace` records and `claims` citing exact quantitative metrics.
2. **Explicitly Bounded**: Clear separation between *Measured Fact*, *AI Prediction*, and *Pathologist Diagnosis*.
3. **Calibrated & Uncertainty-Aware**: Distinguishing model confidence from diagnostic certainty, and factoring in normalized Shannon entropy and OOD status.
4. **Guarded Against Diagnostic Overclaims**: Enforced by `EvidenceValidator`, blocking prohibited phrases (`"confirmed cancer"`, `"definitive diagnosis"`, etc.) and class hallucinations.
5. **Auditable & Consistent**: Unified across FastAPI backend, Android Jetpack Compose UI, MedGemma Pathologist Copilot, and PDF reports.

## 2. Explanation Architecture & Data Flow

```
[Uploaded H&E Image]
        │
        ├─► Optical Quality Check (blur, contrast, brightness)
        ├─► U-Net Gland Morphometry (count, circularity, area, aspect ratio)
        ├─► HoVer-Net Nuclear Phenotyping (count, mean area, perimeter, circularity)
        ├─► Digepath ViT-L/16 Visual Embedding (1024D)
        │
        ▼
[Multimodal Fusion Net (1024D + 16D ──► 9-Class Logits)]
        │
        ├─► [Temperature Calibration (T=1.25)] ──► Calibrated Confidence & Shannon Entropy
        ├─► [AgreementEngine] ──► 5-Channel Consensus & Discordance Analysis
        ├─► [ReferenceMatcher] ──► Curated Cohort Vector Similarity (%)
        ├─► [RegionAnalyzer] ──► 2x2 Spatial Grid Prioritization (R_01 .. R_04)
        │
        ▼
[EvidenceBuilder: Builds Canonical case_result.json]
        │
        ├─► [evidence_trace: Machine-readable key-value dictionary of all factual metrics]
        ├─► [EvidenceGroundedExplainer.generate_claims(): Atomic verifiable claim records]
        ├─► [EvidenceGroundedExplainer.generate_explanation(): Narrative with decision-support phrasing]
        │
        ▼
[EvidenceValidator (Anti-Hallucination Gatekeeper)]
        │
        ├──► If Valid ──► explanation.validated = True, emitted to Client
        └──► If Invalid ──► explanation.validated = False, errors logged & returned with limitation
```

## 5. Uncertainty & Out-of-Distribution Interpretability

1. **Confidence $\neq$ Correctness**: Explanations explicitly state: *"The model assigned X% confidence"* rather than stating that the tissue is definitely the assigned class.
2. **Temperature Calibration**: Raw logits are scaled with $T=1.25$ to curb overconfidence.
3. **Shannon Entropy**: Normalized entropy ($H / \ln 9$) is reported as a quantitative dispersion score ($0.0 \to 1.0$).
4. **Energy-Based OOD Detection**: Specimens falling outside training distribution are flagged as `OOD_DETECTED`, mandating manual pathologist evaluation.
5. **Optical Quality Degradation**: Low contrast or blurred images trigger explicit reliability warnings without false reassurance.

---

## 6. Model Consensus & Reference Similarity Semantics

- **Consensus Agreement**: The `AgreementEngine` scores concordance across 5 distinct evidence channels (Multimodal Fusion, Digepath Foundation Embedding, Nuclear Cytopathology, Gland Architecture, and Curated Cohort Match). Disagreements are explicitly itemized in `discordant_sources`.
- **Reference Cohort Similarity**: $43.9\%$ similarity indicates *histological vector closeness* in 16D+1024D embedding space, and is strictly documented as **not equivalent to a $43.9\%$ probability of disease**.

## 7. Pathologist Copilot Capability & Evidence Boundaries

The Pathologist Copilot (`MedGemmaVLM`) enforces strict domain capability boundaries:

| Clinical Domain | Available Image Evidence | Permitted Copilot Response | Decline / Limitation Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cytopathology** | HoVer-Net nuclear segmentation & counts | Explains nuclear density, area, pleomorphism | Declines specific chromatin ultrastructure not visible on 20x patch |
| **Glandular Architecture** | U-Net gland segmentation | Explains gland circularity, crowding, branching | Declines depth of invasion beyond visible patch mucosa |
| **Prediction Consensus** | Multimodal Fusion & AgreementEngine | Explains calibrated probability & source consensus | Declines definitive diagnostic claims |
| **Molecular / IHC / MSI** | NONE (Standard H&E patch only) | Declines determination; recommends IHC/MSI testing | Explicitly states: *"MSI/IHC status cannot be determined from H&E alone."* |
| **Invasion Criteria** | Mucosa / muscularis in visible field | Explains defining criteria (breach of muscularis mucosae) | Recommends microscopic correlation by pathologist to assess depth |
